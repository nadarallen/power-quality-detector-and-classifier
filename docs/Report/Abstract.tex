\chapter*{Abstract}

Here, try to write the abstract in 12 to 15 lines. An abstract is a self-contained, short, and powerful statement that describes a larger work. Components vary according to discipline. An abstract of a social science or scientific work may contain the scope, purpose, results, and contents of the work. An abstract is a brief summary of a work. It is often used to help the reader quickly ascertain the report's purpose and work done. 

Give the importance of your domain first. Mention techniques that are used in this domain. List what the different possible techniques are. Write what are limitations of other technique? Write why a particular technique(s) is useful and used in this report. Describe your work in this report in 2 or 5 lines.
